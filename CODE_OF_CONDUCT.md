# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Argue about the work, not the person.

## What we expect

Technical disagreement is welcome and useful — this tool makes empirical claims
about federal rulemaking, and those claims should be contested. Do it with
evidence.

Not welcome: harassment, personal attacks, demeaning comments about anyone's
identity or background, publishing others' private information, or sustained
disruption of discussion.

This project touches politically charged rulemakings. Analysis and criticism of
agency conduct is the point. Using the project's spaces to attack individuals,
or to campaign rather than to build, is not.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, and anywhere someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to **info@neatlabs.ai**. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts, and will
explain the reasoning where doing so doesn't compromise a reporter.
